PAW NEST – Pet Boarding Terms & Conditions
By booking our pet boarding service, the pet owner/guardian agrees to the following terms and conditions:

PAW NEST – পোষ্য বোর্ডিং শর্তাবলী
আমাদের পোষ্য বোর্ডিং পরিষেবা বুক করার মাধ্যমে, পোষ্যের মালিক/অভিভাবক নিম্নলিখিত শর্তাবলীতে সম্মত হচ্ছেন:

  1. Advance Booking

    Boarding requests must be made at least 7 days in advance, subject to availability.

  2. Vaccination Requirement

    A valid and up-to-date Vaccination Record/Book must be submitted before check-in. PAW NEST reserves the right to refuse admission if vaccination records are incomplete.

  3. Guardian Identification

    A photocopy of the Guardian's Aadhaar Card must be submitted during check-in. The copy will be securely retained only for the boarding period and returned upon the pet's check-out, if requested.

  4. Health Condition

    Only healthy pets that are free from contagious or infectious diseases will be accepted. PAW NEST may decline admission if a pet appears unwell.

  5. Medication

    If the pet requires medication, the owner must provide the medicines along with clear written instructions regarding dosage and timing.

  6. Food & Special Care

    Owners must inform PAW NEST in advance about any special dietary requirements, allergies, medical conditions, or behavioral concerns.

  7. Emergency Contact

    The owner must provide at least one active emergency contact number that remains reachable throughout the boarding period.

  8. Video Updates

    PAW NEST will provide two video call updates per day to help owners stay connected with their pets.

  9. Exclusive Boarding

    To ensure personalized attention and a stress-free environment, only one pet is accommodated at a time.

  10. Liability

    PAW NEST will take every reasonable precaution to ensure the safety and well-being of your pet. However, the owner acknowledges that unforeseen medical emergencies or incidents may occur despite proper care.

  11. Payment

    Boarding charges and any applicable advance payment must be completed as per the agreed booking terms.

  12. Acceptance of Terms

    By confirming a booking, the owner acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to all the above Terms & Conditions.

  3. অভিভাবকের পরিচয়পত্র

    PAW NEST আপনার পোষ্যের নিরাপত্তা ও সুস্থতা নিশ্চিত করতে যথাসাধ্য সতর্কতা অবলম্বন করবে। তবে মালিক স্বীকার করেন যে, সঠিক যত্ন সত্ত্বেও অপ্রত্যাশিত চিকিৎসাগত জরুরি অবস্থা বা ঘটনা ঘটতে পারে।
